JIC TEMPLE / ORGANIZATION

Purpose, responsibility,
and a record you can read.

The public organization behind a shared spiritual vision.

01

Our filed purpose

The formation document describes a decentralized spiritual system that seeks to awaken human potential through love and intentional creation. It places religious, educational, and charitable purposes at the center of the organization.

02

Participation & governance

Spiritual participation is open. Corporate governance is a separate responsibility. The bylaws text describes a board-managed corporation without statutory members. Participation in faith nodes, digital spaces, or a DAO does not itself grant corporate voting rights.

03

A practice of conscience

The bylaws text protects freedom of conscience and does not require exclusive belief. Learning, reflection, service, and collaboration are ways to participate. No one needs to abandon another tradition to explore JIC.

04

Technology in service of purpose

Digital identities, agents, and shared spaces are tools for connection and creation. Beliefs about consciousness are presented as spiritual inquiry. They are not promises of medical outcomes, scientific proof, investment returns, or immortality.
